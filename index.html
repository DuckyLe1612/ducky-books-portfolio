The chief accountant did not believe me for the first few weeks.

For the first few weeks she checked my figures against the tax office's own portal. They matched. Only then did she move companies across.

What won her over was not accuracy but that nobody had to type any more. Before the software someone always did: her own evenings, people at the practice paid to type, and for a while me. I did the data entry myself to learn the work. I felt I learned less than I hoped, and it took a great deal of time. That is the job I set out to remove.

Twice since, she has overruled me on my own measurements, and both times she was right.

  • I built a counterparty reader for bank statement lines, measured first: 2,466 suggestions, 18 wrong. She said do not switch it on, and she was right: all 18 mistakes happened while the system was fully self-consistent, so no confidence threshold would have caught the first, the only one that matters. I switched off the learning too, collecting nothing for a feature she declined.
  • A separate measurement: 91,278 posted lines, 49 companies. On 54,367, 59.6%, the software had already chosen the account she would have. She still reads every invoice once: "if I posted the whole batch on its suggestion and one was wrong, redoing it would cost a lot of time." Fair: my measurement covers what it gets right, not what one wrong entry costs to unwind. I stopped trying to remove the check and started making it faster.

Both counted on the working system, 5 September 2026.

Not on my own. Nine people have worked in the system, the work theirs as much as mine.
