## Why not the two sources first suggested

- **YouTube Audio Library.** Its help page covers use in videos **on YouTube** and says
  nothing about hosting the video elsewhere. This video sits on GitHub Pages. A licence
  that is silent on a use is not a licence for it.
- **Apple Loops.** The licence is clear and would allow it: royalty-free use in one's own
  compositions, which may then be distributed. But the loops ship inside GarageBand and
  Logic, so it needs a Mac. This was built on Windows.

## Processing

Trimmed to 75.6 seconds, 2 second fade in, 4 second fade out from 71.6 seconds.
Loudness normalised with a two pass `loudnorm` to I=-18 LUFS, TP=-1.0 dBTP, LRA=11.
Measured after encoding: -18.33 LUFS integrated, -4.65 dBTP true peak.
